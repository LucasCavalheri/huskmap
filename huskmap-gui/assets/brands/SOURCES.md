## Missing / not vendored

- `cargo`: no distinct Cargo mark in Simple Icons. Use `rust.svg`.

## Notes

- `aider.svg` is a 436x436 filled square with the mark knocked out (not 24x24,
  not transparent around the mark). It renders heavier than the others.
- Simple Icons has no `xai` or `grok` slug; the Grok mark comes from LobeHub Icons.
- `openai.svg` and `playwright.svg` were removed from later Simple Icons
  releases (v16 and v13). Removals like these usually follow a brand-guidelines
  request, so check the owners' current brand terms before shipping them.
